    How to add it in Canva

    1. Click Copy link above.
    2. In Canva, click the old countdown once and press Delete.
    3. Click an empty spot on the same page, then paste: Ctrl+V (or ⌘+V on a Mac).
    4. Your countdown appears. Drag it into place and resize it from a corner.

    How to change it later

    1. Open your edit link. Your choices are already filled in.
    2. Make your changes and click Copy link.
    3. In Canva, delete the old countdown and add the new link the same way as before, then publish your website again.

    The countdown runs to the wedding moment in the time zone you chose, so it’s right for guests anywhere. Want to change something later? Make a new link here and swap it in.
